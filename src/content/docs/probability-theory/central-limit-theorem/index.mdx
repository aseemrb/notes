The Central Limit Theorem (CLT) describes the universal Gaussian behavior of properly normalized sums of independent random variables. Where the [Laws of Large Numbers](../laws-of-large-numbers) say the sample average concentrates around the mean, the CLT identifies the *fluctuation* around the mean as Gaussian on the scale of $1/\sqrt{n}$.
